Agency: an AI skill for product managers

A skill that gives your AI assistant the method from Sara Capra's book Agency: A Practical Guide for Product Managers to Build Confidence, Navigate Change and Lead with Impact (Avocet Books, 2026).

Describe a real situation, such as a hard stakeholder update, a stretch of self-doubt, a promotion conversation, or a meeting you need to prepare, and your assistant applies the book's frameworks to your context. The skill distills the book's method into instructions. It does not reproduce the book, which has the stories, research, and full exercises behind each tool.

What it covers





Alignment: self-awareness, values and purpose, life-first career design, imposter patterns, and an empowered mindset



Altitude and focus: seeing the whole system, setting priorities, and catching burnout early



Communication: managing early attachment to plans and preparing meetings and updates with intention



Advocacy: making your team's work and your own impact seen



Install

Command line (Claude Code, Cursor, and other coding agents that use the skills CLI):

npx skills add [github-user]/[repo] --skill agency-for-product-leaders -g

Claude.ai or Claude Desktop: download the .zip from [add link], then upload it under Settings > Capabilities > Skills. Menu labels may change; see Anthropic's help center for current steps.

Use it

Describe your situation and mention the skill, or just describe the situation and let your assistant load it. For example:





"Our VP is still anchored to the original launch date even though we've learned a lot. Draft an update using the agency skill."



"Help me prepare a 45-minute cross-functional workshop where I need a decision on scope using the agency skill."
